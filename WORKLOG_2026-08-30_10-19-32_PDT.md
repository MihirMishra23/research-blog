Clarify that the site is a single-author personal blog and define firm boundaries for AI assistance with editorial content.

## Completed

- Added a single-author editorial ownership policy to the product spec.
- Reframed the README milestone as an owner publishing guide rather than contributor onboarding.
- Explicitly removed contributor accounts, public submissions, collaborative editing, role management, and browser-based editorial tooling from scope.
- Established that Mihir writes all publishable article-body prose.

## Design decisions

- The repository and publishing workflow are optimized for one owner, not a multi-author publication.
- Article authorship covers the substantive prose: explanations, arguments, examples, transitions, and conclusions.
- AI can help organize and operate the knowledge system without becoming the voice of the articles.
- Existing sample fixtures can continue to exercise rendering and navigation, but they are not considered finished owner-authored articles.
