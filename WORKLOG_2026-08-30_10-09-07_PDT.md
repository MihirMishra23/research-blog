## Design decisions

### Prioritize exploration before chronology

The introduction is intentionally compact, and the complete interactive map follows it immediately. Exploration routes, recent posts, and notebook context appear only after the graph and its semantic representation.

### Keep V1 exploration controls honest

The three controls are links, not simulated modes. Each is labeled “Initial route” and states what it opens. Frontier exploration begins at a real active topic's open questions instead of implying that a global frontier visualization exists.

### Derive recent writing from canonical content

The homepage sorts published entries by date and takes at most five. Only two posts currently exist, so both are displayed; the section will expand automatically as more posts are published.

### Reuse the map rather than fork it

The homepage, focused route, and QA preview all instantiate the same typed component. Interaction, accessibility, base-path handling, and future map changes therefore remain synchronized.

### Preserve the established publishing platform

This is an existing Astro site deployed through GitHub Pages. Step 12 preserves that architecture and deployment workflow rather than adding a second hosting platform.
