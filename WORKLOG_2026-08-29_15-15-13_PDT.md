## Design decisions

### Make bubbles structural, not decorative

Topic outlines are real SVG node geometry used by connector calculations, interaction states, focus treatment, and hit targets. They are not background decorations placed behind the existing labels.

### Preserve the hand-drawn visual language

All nodes reuse the deterministic asymmetric oval generator. Topics therefore feel related to the larger category bubbles without becoming polished UI pills.

### Give labels explicit breathing room

Each topic's authored width and height now includes visible internal padding. Long names receive proportionally wider bubbles instead of relying on label paint to mask intersecting edges.

## Outcome
