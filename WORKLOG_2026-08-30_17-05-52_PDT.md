## Design decisions

### Use nine existing categories

The current schema already covers the desired release. Avoiding category changes keeps Action Item 1.1 about editorial scope rather than prematurely changing rendering and area geometry.

### Target 36 concepts

Thirty-six concepts are enough to represent the field across the selected areas and expose real layout constraints. The hard cap also prevents scope from expanding into an encyclopedia before the automatic-layout work is evaluated.

### Reserve Interpretability

Interpretability remains important, but a useful treatment needs several mutually connected concepts. It is better deferred as a coherent area than represented by a token node disconnected from the release's systems-oriented path.

### Separate mechanisms from objectives

Post-training covers feedback and optimization mechanisms; Safety covers risk-control objectives, adversarial testing, and runtime enforcement. Overlap must be expressed through relationships or a shared canonical page rather than duplicated topics.

### Keep candidate names provisional

Action Item 1.1 fixes coverage slots, not content IDs. Action Item 1.2 may merge or rename candidates after primary-source review, as long as it respects the 36-concept budget and records any area shift.
