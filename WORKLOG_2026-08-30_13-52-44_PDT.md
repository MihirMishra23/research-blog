## Design decisions

### Keep discovery derived from canonical content

Writing and topic Markdown remain the source of truth. Pagefind indexes the generated topic and published-article pages, while RSS explicitly applies production visibility even when its endpoint is evaluated outside a production build.

### Exclude implementation fixtures

`content-preview`, `map-preview`, and `rendering-test` remain useful development routes, but they are excluded from Pagefind and the sitemap so they cannot appear as public discovery results.

### Centralize deploy-aware URLs

Canonical, RSS, social-image, icon, and Pagefind bundle URLs are generated from the central site configuration. The web manifest is an Astro endpoint rather than a hard-coded public file, so a future custom-domain switch does not leave stale `/research-blog/` paths.

### Preserve a static architecture

Search uses a client-side query against Pagefind's generated static index. RSS, sitemap, metadata, and assets are fully generated during the build; no search server, CMS, analytics service, or account system was introduced.

### Keep editorial content untouched

This step changes discovery and presentation metadata only. It does not generate or rewrite article-body prose.
