## Design decisions

### Prioritize taxonomy before mechanics

The expanded taxonomy supplies the real density, labels, categories, and relationships needed to evaluate layout. Choosing a graph framework against the six-node demonstration would optimize for an artificial problem.

### Prefer deterministic build-time layout

The plan treats automatic layout as a maintainability tool, not a reason to replace the static architecture. The target is deterministic generated geometry with authored node and edge overrides, not a client-side force simulation.

### Preserve intentional edges

Automatic placement does not authorize automatic semantic relationships. Topic metadata remains canonical, every edge must have a named meaning, and exceptional routes can retain authored waypoints to avoid labels and unrelated bubbles.

### Layer advanced interaction carefully

Pan and zoom come before subgraphs, subgraphs before traversal, and sourced timeline metadata before an evolution UI. Each layer must remain keyboard-accessible, reduced-motion-safe, deep-linkable where meaningful, and backed by the semantic text view.

### Keep editorial authorship unchanged

The plan may use AI assistance for topic names, taxonomy, relationships, layout, code, schemas, and tooling. It does not authorize AI-generated publishable article-body prose.
