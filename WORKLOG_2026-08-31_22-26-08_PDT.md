## Design decisions

1. **Measure conservative envelopes.** Axis-aligned bubble bounds and a 16-unit edge clearance deliberately catch routes that may visually interfere with labels.
2. **Do not invent stress-test coordinates.** Addition failure is recorded from the actual approved drafts and V1 validation rather than arbitrary manual placement.
3. **Keep taxonomy and geometry separate.** Uneven area counts are approved content; fixed canvas, missing area layouts, and clearance violations are geometry defects.
4. **Use constraints, not a preferred algorithm.** Action 2.1 establishes spacing, routing, density, label-fit, hit-target, and dynamic-canvas requirements without deciding 2.2 prematurely.
5. **Keep mobile pan/zoom in Section 3.** Section 2 must preserve readability and native overflow; it does not pull advanced viewport controls forward.
