## Design decisions

### Keep all prose visibly provisional

Every topic and article begins with a rendered “Sample content” or “Sample article” notice. The entries are complete enough to exercise the system, but they do not present themselves as finished surveys, benchmarks, or implementation guidance.

### Limit relationships to concepts that exist in the initial set

The initial graph does not reference uncreated concepts such as PPO, reward models, autoregressive decoding, or vision encoders. This keeps the first content set internally valid. Richer prerequisites and progression can be added when the corresponding topic entries exist.

### Validate semantic references during the build

Frontmatter schemas validate the shape of each file; `assertContentReferences()` validates relationships across files. The preview route calls it during the production build, so broken topic or article IDs fail deployment rather than silently generating dead navigation.

### Use a temporary preview instead of preempting permanent routes

Permanent `/writing/[slug]/` and `/topics/[topic]/` routes belong to action items 8 and 9. `/content-preview/` proves that all collection bodies and metadata render now, while keeping those route implementations scoped to their planned steps. It can be removed once both permanent route families exist.

### Defer map geometry to the map-data step

Topic files remain the allowed owner of optional hand-composed positions, but no coordinates were invented here. Action item 10 will define and validate the full editable map model in one coherent pass.

### Verify paper metadata against primary records

Titles, years, and URLs were checked against the corresponding arXiv records. The sample prose remains intentionally brief and paraphrased.
