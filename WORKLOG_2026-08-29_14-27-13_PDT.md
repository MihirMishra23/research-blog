## Design decisions

### Reserve the overview for structure

Only hierarchy and progression edges appear before selection. This makes the initial map explain taxonomy and learning order without mixing in a second relationship type.

### Reveal relationships in context

Selecting a topic reveals only its immediate non-hierarchical relationships as solid red connectors. Their destinations are also named under “Immediate connections” in the detail panel, so color is not the only explanation.

### Stop connectors at real label bounds

Every connector now calculates its endpoint against the destination's elliptical width and height. Wider labels therefore receive wider clearance, preventing lines from continuing beneath topic names.

### Author exceptional routes

Most edges remain deterministic curves. Cross-category relationships that cannot travel cleanly through the radial layout may define explicit waypoints; the FlashAttention-to-Multimodal Models route uses this escape hatch to follow the map's outer edge.
